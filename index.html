Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Something missing? Ask a principal directly on WhatsApp.

Do you keep cars in stock?

No. SUKHANYA is a sourcing service. We search to order, so capital is not tied up in cars on a lot, and your vehicle is matched to your brief.

How is the commission worked out?

We charge a service commission based on the value of the vehicle. The vehicle price, import costs and commission are discussed with you before you decide to proceed.

When do I pay anything?

Only after you have agreed the figures. A field agent then meets you at your location to collect the agreed funds, complete paperwork and start the buying or import process.

Can I choose the country we search?

Yes. Pick China, Japan, the United Kingdom, South Africa or Singapore, name another market in your notes, or ask us to recommend one.

Do you source new and pre-owned?

Both. Tell us which you prefer, or choose open to either, and we will search accordingly.

What if I am not sure what I need?

Select Other or help me choose in the brief, then describe how you will use the vehicle and your budget. A principal will talk it through with you.
